A restaurant loses money every day its doors are closed, so the schedule mattered as much as the tile. We planned the removal, floor preparation and tile in stages across two closed periods, keeping the kitchen out of service for as little time as we could.

The dining room has a textured porcelain with enough grip for busy service. In the kitchen, quarry tile with epoxy grout handles heat, grease and daily washing, and we sloped the tile carefully to the existing floor drains.
