The property manager needed two floors of washrooms refitted without closing the building. Working with the general contractor, we split the job into phases, finishing one washroom on each floor before starting its pair.

Noisy demolition and cutting were kept outside business hours. We checked the backing, drains and fixture blocking with the plumber before setting tile, and handed over each phase on its agreed date. Dense porcelain on the floors and stacked feature walls give the space a cleaner look that's simple for the cleaning staff to maintain.
