These homeowners had just finished a kitchen refresh and wanted a backsplash that would stay in style. They chose a slightly varied glazed tile, so we blended boxes as we went to keep the colour even across the wall.

We centred the layout on the range, ran the offset rows level with the counter and finished the open end with slim brushed trim. Silicone along the counter matches the grout, and the outlets sit flush on box extenders.
