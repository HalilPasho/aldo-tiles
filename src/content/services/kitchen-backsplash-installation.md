## Small area, big effect

A backsplash might only cover 30 square feet, yet it sits at eye level in the room people use most. Because it is small, a crooked line or a sliver of tile beside an outlet has nowhere to hide. Under-cabinet lights make it harder still, since light that skims down the wall throws a shadow from any tile edge that sits proud.

That is why we spend real time on the layout. We find what your eye goes to first, usually the range or the window over the sink, and balance the tile from there. Cabinets and counters in most homes are not perfectly level or square, so we work out how to absorb those small differences where they won't be noticed.

## Choosing a tile you will love for years

Classic subway tile suits nearly any kitchen, and it changes character depending on how you lay it: straight stacked for a modern look, offset for a traditional one, or vertical to make a low wall seem taller. Mosaics and herringbone bring more movement and need more cutting, which adds to the labour. Handmade-look glazed tile has lovely variation from piece to piece, so we blend boxes as we go. Glass needs a white mortar and extra care, because the adhesive shows through.

Think about grout colour too. A matching grout lets the tile surface read as one calm plane, while a contrasting one puts the pattern up front. Bring us a sample tile and we can show you both side by side.

## Details that make it look built in

Most of the finish comes down to the last few centimetres. Outlets get box extenders so that plates sit flush with the new surface. Exposed ends get a slim metal trim or a finished edge, not a raw cut. Where tile meets the counter, we use colour-matched silicone rather than grout, since those two surfaces move independently and hard grout would crack along the joint.

## Living in your home while we work

We know you need your kitchen back. Counters and the range get covered, dust stays controlled, and we clean up before leaving. Many Mississauga homeowners pair a new backsplash with new counters, and if that's your plan, book us after the counters are in so the tile can sit on the finished surface.
